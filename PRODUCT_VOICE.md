# Product voice

**One author.** Ava, an empty state, a transactional email and a marketing page
are the same voice at different volumes. A couple who reads a briefing and then
an invitation should not be able to tell that two people wrote them.

This file is what B3's copy, every future email, and every new surface are
checked against.

## The rules, everywhere

- **One headline sentence. At most three lines beneath. One action.**
- **Numbers, not adjectives.** "14 guests have not replied", never "several".
- **Never a percentage.** Not in progress, not in a briefing, not in a card.
- **No pleasantries, no congratulation, no exclamation marks, no em dashes, no
  "just", no "simply".**
- **Sentence case.** Genuine acronyms (FAQ, RSVP, BYO, OK) are not shouting.
- **US English**, including comments and identifiers.
- **No emoji.** Monochrome text marks that inherit our typeface are not emoji.
- **Ava is "Ava", never "she".** `✦` is Ava's signature and appears nowhere else.

## The rule that outranks the others

**AVA WOULD RATHER BE VISIBLY LIMITED THAN QUIETLY WRONG.**

- A fact this wedding does not have is **stated as not set, with where it would
  live**. Never inferred, never filled from a norm.
- Data that did not load is **named as unseen**, never treated as empty.
  "Nothing needs you today" computed from a failed request is a lie the reader
  cannot detect.
- Anything built from what couples *generally* do, rather than from this
  wedding's data, is a defect.

## Per surface

### Ava

    Ava says          14 guests have not replied.
    Ava does not say  You're doing great — just a few replies to chase!

    Ava says          No budget set. It lives on the Budget page.
    Ava does not say  Most couples spend around $35,000 at this stage.

    Ava says          Your guest list could not be loaded, so this is not
                      the whole picture.
    Ava does not say  Nothing needs you today.   (when a fetch failed)

### Empty states

One line saying what the page is for. One line saying what to do first,
specific to this wedding where the data allows. One action. **No illustration,
no secondary buttons, no tips carousel.**

    Before  No vendors added yet
            Click + Add vendor to start tracking your suppliers
    After   Vendors is where you track everyone you hire.
            Add the venue first. Most other bookings depend on the date
            and place it fixes.
            [ Add vendor ]

### Transactional email

The subject says what happened or what is asked. The body says it once. **An
invitation is a doorway, not a document** — every detail it carries is a reason
the guest does not open the website.

    Before  You're invited: a wedding
            Ceremony 3:00pm, Pura Ulun Danu
            Reception 6:30pm, Villa Serena
            [ RSVP now ]
    After   You're invited: Chris & Sia
            Saturday, 14 June 2027
            [ Open your invitation ]

### Dashboard chrome

State is **"next", never "done"**. No progress bars, no completion counts, no
percentages.

    Before  Planning progress    14 of 38 complete   [====------]
    After   What is left         24 left

### Marketing

The same voice, louder only in typography. No feature-list creep, and the
homepage stays on "nothing else needs you today".

## The test

Read the sentence aloud. If it congratulates, hedges, or claims something the
product cannot know, it fails. If it states a number this wedding actually has,
or names plainly what is missing and where it lives, it passes.
