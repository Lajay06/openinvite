## `rsvpSent` — the approved SHAPE, tails not yet accepted

Only the shape was approved, not nineteen individual lines. Each universe:

1. keeps its **existing `rsvpSent` opening**;
2. then a **neutral clause that never confirms guest-list membership** — the
   sense of *"If that email is on the guest list, we've just sent your personal
   RSVP link — check your inbox and spam folder"*;
3. then a **guest-actionable close** — the sense of *"If nothing arrives, ask
   the couple to add your address"*.

Both new clauses are written **in that universe's register**, not pasted.

Constraints, same as the intros: US English; no loanword flourishes; no
construction used more than twice across the set; capri's exclamation is a
standing exemption; the swap test — a line that would read equally well under
another universe's name has not been written yet.

The neutral clause is not politeness. Confirming that an address IS on the
guest list turns the form into an oracle: anyone could test addresses against
someone's wedding. The phrasing has to be identical whether or not the address
matched.
