# Essentials sweep

Before anything else ships, prove the plain things a couple or a guest expects from any product of this kind. Each line below gets one of: YES with file:line; PARTIAL with one sentence on what is missing; NO. Lines marked (live) are proved by the owner on production and get OWNER. Do not fix anything. Do not infer from names; open the code path. Where a thing exists but a couple could not find it from the obvious page, answer PARTIAL and say where it hides. Finish with a table of every NO and PARTIAL grouped by section, which becomes the next goals.
