# Gifting v2: give Openinvite like a gift, not a coupon (lane B, 2026-10-11)

Runs under the goal autonomy protocol in WORKFLOW.md. Everything in this goal ships behind a flag, GIFTING_V2, off in production until item 9 passes. The current gift flow keeps working until the flag flips.

## What we are building

A giver buys Pro or Ultra for a couple inside a flow that is the onboarding shell, consumed not forked. They choose the plan, say who it is for, write a note in one of three styles, pick the first line and the photo, pick when it sends, pay on Stripe. The couple get one email with a button. The button opens a claim page; one tap, sign in, and they land in onboarding with the plan already on. No code is typed by anyone.

## Rulings

- The gift flow uses the onboarding components as they are: split shell, white background, coloured logo, left-aligned content column, Select cards, the completion screen. Consume, do not fork. If a component needs a prop, add the prop, keep every existing consumer byte-identical, and say so in the PR body.
- Photo side of the split: step 1 and the done screen show onboarding's own images for its plan step and its completion screen (reuse, no new assets). Steps 2 to 6 show the live email preview instead of a photo, rendered by the same template the email is sent from, so what the giver sees is what the couple get.
- Naming: the guest-facing product is the Guest Suite, described as the guest experience. The words "wedding website" never appear in anything a giver, a couple or a guest reads.
- Copy below is verbatim. US English. No em dashes. Nothing in a Pro email or page names the Guest Suite, RSVP, song requests or registry.
- Fonts in email: Gmail does not load web fonts. The plain note is live text in Plus Jakarta Sans with Helvetica and Arial fallbacks. Serif (Cormorant Garamond, italic) and handwriting (Caveat) notes are rendered as an image through Cloudinary's text overlay at 2x for retina, 520px wide, with the full note as alt text. The portal preview uses the same rendered image.
- Photos: hosted in the Openinvite Cloudinary, delivered f_auto, q_auto, w_1200, ar_6:5, c_fill, g_auto. Owner may add three more ids later; the picker reads from one list in one file.
- The claim link carries a signed single-use token. The token is never stored; its HMAC-SHA256 (same key and function as collaboratorAuth's hashId) is. No promotion code is minted for v2 gifts.
- Payments: api/webhooks/ is frozen. The three payments-adjacent changes (items 6, 7, 8) are separate HELD PRs with the Payments-Change trailer, each waiting for the advisor's merge line. scripts/check-payments-freeze.mjs is never edited.
- No deletions of the v1 gift code until the owner says so after item 9; v1 stays reachable while the flag is off.

## Schema (owner adds in Base44 chat; lane prints the text at item 0 and stops)

Add to PlanGift, all optional, all written only by the server:
- recipient_names_enc: AES-256-GCM ciphertext of how the couple are addressed, same scheme as recipient_email_enc.
- headline_key: string enum nice_start, for_you, enjoy_this, spreadsheet, sorted. Plain.
- photo_key: string, the Cloudinary public id from the allowed list. Plain.
- note_style: string enum plain, serif, hand. Plain.
- send_at: date-time, when the reveal email should go; null means immediately. Plain.
- sent_at: date-time, when the reveal email went. Plain.
- claim_token_hash: string, HMAC-SHA256 hex of the claim token. Plain hash.
- claimed_at: date-time. Plain.
- claimed_user_id_hash: string, HMAC-SHA256 hex of the claiming user's id. Plain hash.
- edit_token_hash: string, HMAC-SHA256 hex of the buyer's edit token (change address or note until claimed). Plain hash.
- version: integer, 2 for this flow; v1 rows have none.
RLS unchanged. The lane mirrors the fields in a held PR after the owner confirms.

## The giver's flow, seven pages at /gift (flagged)

1. Which plan. Onboarding's plan step, two cards, Pro US$49 and Ultra US$99, Select on each. No trial line. Heading: Give them the whole thing. Sub: One payment. Theirs for 24 months. Nothing for them to pay, ever. Pro card line: The planner, complete. Guest list, budget, seating, vendors, schedule, to-dos, and Ava. Ultra card line: Everything in Pro, plus the Guest Suite: the guest experience, from one link, no accounts, no app.
2. Who it is for. Heading: Who is the lucky couple? Fields: How should we address them? (help: Exactly as it should read at the top of their email.) Their email (help: The gift goes here. You can change it until they open it.) Your name, as they know it.
3. Your note. Heading: Say the thing. Textarea, 600 characters, help: It goes in their email exactly as you write it. Style control labelled Written in, three choices: Plain, Serif, Handwriting, each rendered in its own face. Preview updates on every keystroke (debounced 300 ms for the rendered styles).
4. The first line. Heading: The first line they read. Five Select cards, one per headline, each showing the headline rendered with the names typed in step 2. Default nice_start.
5. The photo. Heading: Pick the picture at the top. Sub: It sits above your note. Choose the one that feels like them. Grid from the list above, same tile style as onboarding's universe grid, label under each, default The boxes.
6. When and pay. Heading: When should it land? Two cards: Now (Within a minute of your payment.) and On a date (Pick a morning. We send it at 9 am where they are.) with a date field when chosen; timezone from the browser, shown as text so the giver can see it. Summary rows: Gift, For, To, From, Total. Fine print: You pay on Stripe. Your receipt comes from Stripe; everything else comes from us. Refund any time until they open it. Button: Pay US$99 and send (or US$49; AUD prices where the existing pricing resolver says so).
7. Done. Completion screen shape. Heading: That was generous of you, {giver}. Body: It is on its way to {email}. We have sent you a copy, with their claim link in case you want to hand it over in person. Box titled What happens next: {names} open the email, tap Open your gift, sign in with Google, Apple or email, and they are inside with {Plan} already on. Nothing to type, nothing to pay. Second line: If it has not arrived in an hour, forward your copy to them; the link works either way. Links: Change the address or the note. Give another.

Draft state lives in sessionStorage under one key until payment; nothing is written to PlanGift before Stripe confirms.

## The claim page, /gift/claim/:token (flagged)

Onboarding shell. Photo side: the chosen photo. Content: the headline the giver chose, the body line, the note card, button Open your gift, under it: We will ask for your names and a date. No card, nothing to enter. Tapping the button runs sign in (Google, Apple, email, the existing auth), then calls the claim endpoint; on success the plan is set, the gift is marked claimed, and the couple land on onboarding step 1 with a single line at the top: A gift from {giver}. {Plan}, paid for, 24 months. Already signed in: skip straight to the claim. Already on a plan: Pro gift to Pro account or Ultra to Ultra extends by 24 months; Ultra gift to Pro account upgrades and extends; Pro gift to Ultra account shows: You already have Ultra, which is more than this gift. Reply to this email and we will sort it out with {giver}. and the gift stays unclaimed. Expired or used token: a plain page, This link has already been used. If that is a surprise, reply to the email it came in and we will look.
