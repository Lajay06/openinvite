What the mockup is not hiding

CONSTRAINTS, NOT DECORATION

Four things about the journey data, including one correction to an earlier version of this page. A mockup that smoothed these over would be selling a layer that cannot be built as drawn.

1. Corrected 19 August — “Set your budget” works today. An earlier version of this mockup said this step could never complete, because isComplete reads wedding.budget.total while budget is AES ciphertext at rest. That claim was wrong. AvaStudio passes the record from getMyWeddingDetails() — the server-decrypting endpoint — so the step receives {total: 154000} and completes correctly. The false reading came from evaluating the function against a record fetched raw from Base44, which is not what the function is given.

1b. What the audit did establish. Of 55 places that read the wedding record, 53 use the decrypting reader and 2 read WeddingDetails raw. Next up must use the decrypting one, and that will be pinned in CI — because a raw read makes the budget step report incomplete forever, silently, and it looks exactly like a couple who has not set a budget. The daily update already loads correctly, so this variant inherits the right source; the pin exists to stop a later refactor breaking it.

2. Three of seven steps are Ultra-only — build website, turn on RSVP, publish website. A free-plan couple sees Next up point at something they cannot do. The mockup marks them Ultra rather than hiding them, but the real decision is whether Next up should ever surface a gated step as the next thing, or route around it and say why.

3. The journey reaches 5 of 44 planner pages. Moodboard, styling, beauty, food & beverage, photography, vows, favours, honeymoon, registry, transport, accommodation and the whole Guest Suite have no orientation story at all. Next up will answer “what now?” for the launch path and stay silent everywhere else — which is honest, but it is not the same as orienting the product.

Also visible above: “Publish your website” reads complete while “Build your website” does not — the fixture has a live site with two of three core pages empty. The steps have no ordering constraint between them, so Next up can legitimately propose publishing something unbuilt.
